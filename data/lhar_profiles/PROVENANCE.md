Pillar layouts (Yim 2020 ESI Table S2): v1a inter-distance 28 um, v1b 49 um (standard), v2a 98 um, all 4 um diameter, hexagonal; channel opening widths 80-100 um (0.5-5 mm channels; ESI Table S1). The four supplements (Yim 2020, Yim 2022, Jarvilehto 2023, Gonsalves 2024) contain no tabulated measured profiles; Yim 2020 Figs. 7-9 and 11 and ESI Figs. S4, S7 are raster plots of the profiles whose front position and slope Table 1 already tabulates, so they are not digitised. Arts 2019 Fig. 3a (H2O-limited profiles at three temperatures) is not digitised: it concerns the water step only. The 2026 ZnO paper (doi 10.1116/6.0004982) reports one usable profile (sample B, 200 C: x50/H = 680, fitted c = 0.002); its quoted slope (0.00058) is in units the text does not define and is not used.
