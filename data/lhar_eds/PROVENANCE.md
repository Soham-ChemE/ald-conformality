Tescan Mira3 SEM with Thermo Scientific EDS. "An accelerating voltage of 5 keV was used for the SEM-EDS line scan."
"The length of the line was 500 µm with measurement points at 1 µm intervals. Outliers due to pillars and edges in
surface channels were removed from the elemental profile results. The line scans are averages of five measurements."
Top membrane was peeled off before the measurement. Results text: at 5 kV the X-ray interaction volume exceeds the
Al2O3 film thickness, so Si substrate signal is present along the whole scan.

## Digitisation method

Neither figure has vector paths: `page.get_drawings()` returns nothing for the plots; each is a single embedded
raster (S8: xref 105, 726 x 438 px; Fig. 5: xref 250, 807 x 1181 px, panel (b) is the lower part, visibly resampled/blurred).
The embedded images were extracted at native resolution (no re-rendering).

1. Pixels classified by nearest reference colour (trace colours, white, gridline grey, black), distance limit 70 (S8) / 60 (5b) in RGB.
2. Per pixel column, the vertical run of the trace colour is found; the value is the midpoint of that run
   (stroke centre; equivalent to a column median for a solid stroke). If several runs exist, the one continuous with the previous column is used.
3. Where a later-drawn trace touches the run and the visible part is thinner than the normal stroke, the visible
   outer edge plus half the stroke thickness is used, and the point is flagged.
4. One point per pixel column: 0.911 um per column in S8, 0.804 um per column in 5(b). The original data are at
   1 um steps (about 500 points), so the CSVs slightly oversample the original; neighbouring points are not independent.

### Error estimate
- x: about +/-0.5 um calibration, plus +/-0.5 px column quantisation (0.46 um S8, 0.40 um 5b). Total about +/-1 um.
- y: stroke-centre uncertainty about +/-0.5 to 1 px, i.e. +/-5 to 10 a.u. in S8 and +/-9 to 18 a.u. in 5(b). The strokes are 5 to 6 px thick (50 to 60 a.u. in S8, about 80 a.u. in 5b), so point-to-point noise narrower than the stroke is partly merged.
- Independent cross-check: the S8 V0031 trace and the 5(b) Al trace are the same scan (sample 11). After mapping x_S8 = x_5b + 255.0 um (best fit, +/-0.3 um) the two digitisations differ by rms 9.5 a.u. on the flat parts, 11.8 a.u. overall, mean offset +4.9 a.u. (S8 higher), worst single point 65 a.u. (on a steep flank).
- Flagged points and the baseline of the two lower S8 traces are worse: see oddities.

## Channel entrance position

- Fig. 5(b): not marked on the axes of panel (b). The Region I to IV brackets are drawn under the SEM image in panel (a) only (scale bar 100 µm), for the right-hand channel. The text states: "In front of the LHAR channel (Region I of Fig. 3b), at a range of ca. -40 to 40 µm (Fig. 5b), a constant film thickness was observed." So the scan crosses the central opening with one LHAR channel on each side, entrances at about x = -40 and +40 um. The SEM image in (a) carries the chip label "W80 L0.5mm", i.e. 80 um opening width. In the digitised Al trace the step from the lower open-area level to the higher in-channel level is centred at -41.5 and +37.5 um (+/-1.5 um), 79 um apart, so the profile is offset about -2 um from the printed zero.
- Fig. S8: not marked and not stated. The same steps are present in all three traces at about x = 213 and 291.5 um (V0057: 214.5 / 291.0; V0069: 211.5 / 291.5; V0031: 213.0 / 292.0), which by analogy with Fig. 5(b) are the two channel entrances (inferred, not given by the authors).
- Half-maximum positions of the outer flanks (plateau/2): V0057 96.6 and 409.1 um; V0069 73.0 and 430.0 um; V0031 113.0 and 391.8 um; Fig. 5(b) Al -141.9 and 136.3 um.

## Oddities

- Legend/sample mismatch in Fig. S8: the caption says Table 1 Series D (samples 10, 8, 11 = V0057, V0001, V0031), but the legend's middle trace is "V0069". In ESI Table 1, V0069 is sample 2 (Series A, wafer M10-F03, layout 1B, 500 nm, 500 cycles), not sample 8 (V0001). The 500-cycle trace is therefore probably a different chip from the reflectometer Series D sample; file named `..._as_labelled`.
- The V0069 trace is wider than the other two (flank half-maxima 357 um apart, against 312 um for 250 cycles and 279 um for 1000 cycles). Coated width does not vary monotonically with cycle number.
- Fig. S8 V0031 and Fig. 5(b) Al are the same data plotted with x origins 255 um apart (not 250).
- Overlap in Fig. S8: draw order is yellow, gold, orange (top). Along the baseline (x below about 60 to 100 um and above about 410 to 440 um) the orange trace covers the other two. There the yellow and gold traces are visible only as thin slivers: 415 and 469 points survive out of about 550 columns, with gaps up to 16 um, and the surviving baseline points are all flagged. Their baseline values are only constrained to lie within the orange stroke (about 18 +/- 30 a.u.). Yellow is also partly hidden on both steep flanks where it runs alongside orange (x about 96 to 103 and 402 to 418 um). Orange/gold crossings at about x = 114 to 117 and 388 to 392 um leave short gaps in gold.
- Overlap in Fig. 5(b): draw order O, Al, Si (top). Al is touched by Si only at the crossings near x = -139 to -119 and 114 to 136 um (20 flagged points) and at one spot near 14 um; no gaps. O is hidden by Al along the baseline and on parts of the flanks (gaps up to 10 um).
- The mid-grey x-axis line in Fig. 5(b) has almost the same colour as the Si trace; it is excluded from the Si mask below the 1000 a.u. gridline (Si never goes below about 1320 a.u.).
- No clipped axes: all traces lie inside the plotted ranges and the data span the full 500 um scan.
- Fig. 5(b) image is low resolution (17.9 a.u. per pixel); for the 1000-cycle Al trace prefer `figS8_Al_V0031_1000cyc.csv` (10 a.u. per pixel, unoccluded) and use the Fig. 5(b) file for the stated entrance position and the Si/O context.
