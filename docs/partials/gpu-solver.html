12 · Run the 3D solver in your browser

The voxel Monte Carlo, on your own graphics card.

Section 11 cross-checked the 1D hole model against a second, independent solver: test-particle Monte Carlo on a 3D voxel grid in the molecular-flow regime (lib/aldmc). This is that solver written as a WebGPU compute shader. Every particle is one GPU thread: it enters the mouth with a cosine distribution, flies in straight lines through the distance field, sticks with probability s (1 − θ) on the face it hits or re-emits diffusely, and the hit counts relax the coverage of every exposed voxel face. Pick a feature, press Run, and watch the floor saturate against the number the paper quotes.

What runs where. The physics is that of lib/aldmc (molecular flow; cosine entry over the open mouth; sticking s w (1 − θ) on the face's own coverage; loss r; diffuse re-emission about the facet normal; escape through the mouth; N₀ h² w sites per face with the staircase area correction w; a step of M particles standing for n v̄/4 Amouth dt molecules; the Langmuir relaxation of the per-face collision count). The shader (assets/aldmc-gpu.wgsl) runs on your graphics card through WebGPU, one compute invocation per particle with its own xoshiro128** stream and atomic per-face counters; a second pass applies the coverage update. If navigator.gpu is missing (WebGPU is in Chrome, Edge and Safari 26, Firefox on Windows and behind a flag elsewhere) the same algorithm runs in plain JavaScript on one CPU thread (assets/aldmc-cpu.js): the same statistics, ten to twenty times slower. Results are statistical: the floor t₉₀ scatters by a few tenths of a per cent between seeds at the reference budgets and more at small M; the time step enters linearly through the coverage frozen within a step (0.6 % at the reference dt). The reference values are the paper's voxel runs at 16 voxels across (paper/wp/wp9_voxel_mc.md, 0.3 Pa TMA, 300 °C, s 5·10⁻³, N₀ 4·10¹⁸ m⁻²). What the demo is not: the film does not narrow the opening as it grows, and there are no gas-phase collisions by construction (Kn ≫ 1 inside the features); the time step scales with N₀, pressure and temperature but not with s, so change dt by hand when you change s.
